# Direction Approval

## Selected direction

Cassette Futurism is the selected identity direction.

User selection:

> The cassette futurism works.
> I quite like the 10‑4 cassette itself, the one with the buttons.

## Presented boards

- Initial direction: `aesthetic-variations/cassette-futurism/concept-board.png`
- Revised icon direction: `aesthetic-variations/cassette-futurism/concept-board-v2.png`

## Requested refinement

Keep the `10.4` monochrome mark, secondary lockup, typography, equipment palette, and cassette applications.
Replace the abstract standalone icon with iconography that reads clearly as a cassette recorder or walkie-talkie.

## Revised icon

The revised icon is a compact pocket radio recorder with a `10.4` channel display, record/stop/play controls, and a short antenna.
It retains the coral tally point as the only active-color accent.

## Final approval

The pocket recorder icon is approved.
The `TEN-FOUR` lettering from the secondary lockup is approved as the name wordmark.
The numeric `10.4` lettering from the monochrome mark is approved with an orange period.

The active application uses the name wordmark in compact sidebar chrome and the numeric wordmark in onboarding.
