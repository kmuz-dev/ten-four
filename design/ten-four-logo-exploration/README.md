# 10.4 Logo Exploration

Status: awaiting direction selection.

These boards explore the new `10.4` identity as a radio acknowledgement and channel reference.
They are visual concept studies, not production vector masters.
The selected direction should be redrawn as deterministic SVG geometry and tested at app-icon and menu-bar sizes before integration.

## Directions

- `channel-check`: segmented channel-display numerals with the decimal point as the live tally lamp.
- `roger-slash`: a transmitting antenna slash that connects the `10` and `4`.
- `squelch-cut`: soft-machined radio-faceplate typography with channel-marker cuts.

## Aesthetic variations

- `aesthetic-variations/cassette-futurism`: angular Japanese consumer-electronics language, molded controls, stencil numerals, and safety-orange signaling.
- `aesthetic-variations/corporate-vaporwave`: an imagined 1990s global telecom identity with orbit geometry, pastel office colors, and corporate italic typography.
- `aesthetic-variations/frutiger-aero`: a glossy humanist system built from air, glass, water, and a coral transmission bead.
- `aesthetic-variations/corporate-cyberspace`: restrained 1990s multimedia typography with transmission echoes and infrared accent colors.
- `aesthetic-variations/y2k-aqua`: translucent consumer-electronics forms, orbital geometry, silver, and ice-blue gel materials.

The boards interpret the visual grammar described by CARI rather than reproducing gallery artifacts.
The reference pages were Cassette Futurism, Corporate Vaporwave, Frutiger Aero, Corporate Cyberspace, and Y2K Aesthetic.

The Cassette Futurism direction was selected for development.
Its revised `concept-board-v2.png` replaces the abstract standalone glyph with a compact pocket radio recorder icon.

## Shared constraints

- Primary identity: `10.4`.
- Secondary spelling: `TEN-FOUR`.
- Palette: warm off-white, graphite, aluminum gray, and tally red `#FF4A26`.
- Radio references should feel contemporary and ownable rather than militaristic or nostalgic.
- Avoid microphones, speech bubbles, ordinary waveforms, tactical badges, and generic AI imagery.

## Selection gate

Do not replace active application branding until a direction or explicit hybrid is selected.
