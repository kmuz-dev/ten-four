# Pass 2 Prompt Record

## Caret Pulse

Create a symbol in which one confident continuous shape begins as a soft vocal pulse and resolves into a crisp text insertion caret.
Use near-black ink with one warm coral accent.
Avoid microphones, speech bubbles, generic waveforms, literal keyboard keys, and AI motifs.

## Press Seal

Express hold-to-talk as a pressure imprint: a compact rounded-square form indented by one circular press, with displaced negative space becoming a small text-like notch.
Use deep ultramarine and warm off-white.
Avoid fingers, microphones, literal keyboard keys, record buttons, and power symbols.

## Quiet Fold

Create one folded ribbon whose left edge is soft and rounded like spoken breath, then folds once and exits as two clean horizontal typographic strokes.
Use deep burgundy and warm ivory.
Avoid document icons, page corners, arrows, speech bubbles, and generic ribbon marks.

## Pressure Field

Start from a compact rounded square, then make one off-center circular pressure point deform the inner negative space toward it while the outer silhouette remains stable.
Use very dark navy and acid lime.
Avoid targets, eyes, cameras, record buttons, power symbols, and keycaps.

## Language Blocks

Interlock exactly three chunky geometric forms into one compact emblem.
Progress from a soft spoken-syllable form to transitional and crisp type-like blocks, using one shared negative-space channel.
Use warm saffron yellow and black.
Avoid lists, equalizers, app grids, chain links, and generic modular marks.
