# Image Generation Prompts

The built-in image-generation tool produced one visual seed per direction.
Each final asset is a deterministic vector reconstruction rather than a traced or embedded bitmap.

## Keystone

Create one original symbol-only logo concept: a tactile hybrid keycap seen from directly above, simplified into a memorable platform-neutral glyph, with one bold outer silhouette, a restrained inset top plane, and a tiny recessed inactive LED socket.
Use graphite and neutral gray only on a transparent background.
Avoid text, letters, permanent red, hands, pink, microphones, waveforms, sparkles, AI orbs, faces, cartoon styling, glossy gaming aesthetics, Apple marks, and keyboard legends.

## Press Plane

Create one original symbol-only logo concept that expresses a key being pressed using two offset geometric planes, with a stable lower frame, a compact shifted upper plane, and a tiny inactive LED socket integrated at their meeting point.
Use graphite and neutral gray only on a transparent background.
Avoid a nested rounded-square construction and all prohibited motifs from the brand spec.

## Tally Cut

Create one original symbol-only logo concept as a monolithic graphite key-like slab whose identity comes from bold corner cuts, controlled negative space, and a recessed inactive light well.
Prioritize one ownable silhouette that survives at 16px.
Avoid nested rounded rectangles, literal keyboard legends, and all prohibited motifs from the brand spec.

## Typeform

Create one original symbol-only logo concept showing spoken language condensing into written letterform strokes, beginning with soft rounded contours and progressively becoming crisp typographic stems and a baseline.
Do not form a recognizable alphabet letter or monogram.
Avoid literal microphones, ordinary waveforms, quotation marks, speech bubbles, and all prohibited motifs from the brand spec.
