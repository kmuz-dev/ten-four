# Symbol Exploration Brand Spec

## Core identity

- The mark is platform-neutral.
- The primary concept is a tactile hybrid of a physical keycap and an abstract glyph.
- The exploration includes three keycap interpretations and one voice-to-letterforms challenger.
- Ownable distinctiveness leads evaluation, with trust and small-size clarity as mandatory gates.

## Color

- Background graphite: `#1B1B1D`
- Surface graphite: `#2C2C2F`
- Raised graphite: `#55555A`
- Light graphite: `#77777D`
- Tally live: `#FF4A26`
- Tally live on dark surfaces: `#FF6242`
- Canvas light: `#F6F5F2`
- Canvas dark: `#1B1B1D`

Idle artwork must not contain either Tally color.
Tally red appears only in recording-state assets.

## Typography

The symbols contain no text or monograms.
Direction boards use SF Pro only for explanatory labels outside the identity artwork.

## Required applications

- Editable transparent mark SVG
- Editable app-icon SVG
- Transparent 1024px mark PNG
- 1024px app-icon PNG
- Launcher-size PNG family
- Tauri platform icon bundle
- Light and dark tray variants for idle, recording, transcribing, and warning
- Direction board and shared comparison board

## Prohibited motifs

- Cartoon hands or faces
- Pink branding
- Microphones
- Ordinary audio waveforms
- Sparkles or AI magic motifs
- Glowing or colored idle LEDs
- H monograms or other name-dependent letterforms
- Apple marks or copied platform trademarks
